Delete your online account

From the game

  1. Open Player profile and find Your online account.
  2. Select Delete online account.
  3. Sign in again with the same Apple or Google account to verify ownership, then confirm deletion.

If you cannot access the game

Email contact@deathmancer.com with the subject Deathmancer account deletion. State which sign-in provider you used and the account email, or your Apple relay address if applicable. We may need to verify ownership before deleting data. Do not send a password, verification code, identity document or payment-card details.

What is removed or retained

Deletion removes your Firebase game account and linked online profile, matchmaking assignments and tables. A shared table is closed for all its participants. Local solo records and local preferences stay on the device unless you remove them separately through the app or device's storage controls.

Deletion does not refund a purchase or automatically erase store or RevenueCat transaction records. These may be retained for ownership restoration, fraud prevention, accounting or legal requirements; contact us about related data requests. Compact game-balance records without account identifiers and aggregate statistics may remain. See the privacy policy for retention details.
